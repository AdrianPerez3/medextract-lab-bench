# Data cards

## Scheduled postoperative HbA1c

Jo Ann Galvan (2026), *Long-Term Clinical Outcomes and Cost-Effectiveness of Bariatric Surgery for Type 2 Diabetes in Malaysia: A 10-Year Retrospective Cohort Study*, Harvard Dataverse, CC0 1.0. Use the v1.0 wide file in the Dataverse tabular download format. The native SPSS file has a different digest. The benchmark accepts tabular SHA-256 `ce595ea3de922e1bf4c68637100144b155e7baeac0baeab052c702702bcca1b6`. [Dataset](https://doi.org/10.7910/DVN/T9OBE3) · [Wide table used by the benchmark](https://dataverse.harvard.edu/api/access/datafile/13955661)

One subject contributes one origin per visit analysis. The primary history is preoperative, 3, 6 and 9 months. The target is the reported 12-month HbA1c on a nominal 0/90/180/270/365-day calendar. Actual collection dates and result-availability timestamps are unavailable. The study reports HbA1c in percent. A specific assay standard cannot be established from that label. Earlier visit pairs and postoperative-only histories are secondary analyses of the same participants.

## Hospital hemoglobin

Barış Gün Sürmeli, René Staritzbichler, Clemens Friedrich Ringel, Saleem Al-Dakkak, Helene Dörksen and Thorsten Kaiser (2025), *Pseudonymized Complete Blood Count Test Data*, Zenodo, CC BY 4.0. Download `KL_dataset.xlsx`, SHA-256 `3f20282943ecaa7ce1936209ec9ad2273a0946da9b492dffc7f79b93f45ceef4`. [Dataset](https://zenodo.org/records/15674541) · [Original workbook](https://zenodo.org/api/records/15674541/files/KL_dataset.xlsx/content) · [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

The laboratory cohort selects repeat patients with complete CBC panels and excludes documented recent transfusions. Collapse repeated measurements on the same released date to their median and index at the fourth distinct day. Predict 90 days ahead. Choose the observed target nearest day 90 within inclusive days 76–104, breaking ties toward the earlier date. Subjects without an in-window target still count in the eligible denominator. The date-shift procedure, result-release clock and out-of-system care limit clinical interpretation. The historical scorecard includes a reference digest for the earlier analysis cache. This implementation reads the original workbook and verifies its own digest.

## External cohort and schema sources

- Johnson et al., MIMIC-IV Clinical Database Demo v2.2. The openly available selected 100-person demo is distributed under ODbL 1.0. Retrieve `hosp/labevents.csv.gz`, `hosp/d_labitems.csv.gz`, the license and SHA-256 list from that release. The package contains their fingerprints and a counts-only protocol. The demo overlaps the full dataset. Exclude these people from a future independent MIMIC performance test. Full MIMIC-IV v3.1 requires PhysioNet credentialing, training and the DUA. [Demo and terms](https://physionet.org/content/mimic-iv-demo/2.2/) · [Full release](https://physionet.org/content/mimiciv/3.1/) · [Lab field definitions](https://mimic.mit.edu/docs/iv/modules/hosp/labevents.html)
- The Japanese luseogliflozin study publishes a longitudinal XLSB supplement with scheduled measured HbA1c. It is a potential medication-initiation transfer study with different selection and intervention. The cohort’s row definition, exclusions, availability assumptions and data-sharing terms belong in a separately versioned protocol. [Original study and S1 data](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0248577)
- GRADE requires a repository request. CALERIE Phase 2 has public documentation and its current access pathway should be checked with the AgingResearchBiobank. The New York bariatric paper describes a comparable schedule; availability of measured HbA1c at the required intermediate visits and access to person-level data need confirmation. [GRADE](https://repository.niddk.nih.gov/study/151) · [CALERIE](https://calerienetwork.org/) · [New York study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32703734/)

The Japanese S1 field names include measured HbA1c at months 0, 1, 3, 6, 9 and 12. Published inclusion and follow-up rules condition on some events after treatment begins. An analysis limited to people who completed treatment would have a different target population from new treatment initiators. Preserve the distinction, confirm one independent person per row and source-specific missingness before use. The supplement has its own CC BY 4.0 record. [Data citation and license](https://plos.figshare.com/articles/dataset/Dataset_in_the_present_study_/14218161)
